Worn siding, loose panels or an exterior you are ready to change? Vermac Construction helps homeowners in Malta, Ballston Spa and surrounding Saratoga County communities plan a siding replacement.

Deciding whether to repair or replace

Cracked panels, recurring loose sections and visible deterioration are reasons to have your exterior assessed. A small damaged area may need a different scope than siding that is worn across the whole house. Describe what you are seeing when you contact us so we can discuss the right next step for your home.

If you have noticed moisture or soft areas around trim, mention those too. An exterior estimate may need to account for conditions underneath the existing siding; some issues only become visible when materials are removed.

What affects the replacement estimate?

  • The size, height and shape of the home.
  • The existing siding and the removal work required.
  • Your material, profile, color and trim choices.
  • The number of windows, doors and other exterior details.
  • Any underlying repair work identified during assessment or removal.

These details are why we provide a project-specific estimate instead of publishing a single price for every house. Tell us your town, what you want to change and your preferred timing when you request a quote.

Plan the details before work begins

Ask how removal, cleanup, access around the home and any unexpected repair work will be handled. If you want to discuss gutters, trim or another part of the exterior at the same time, include that in your message so the team can confirm the scope.
