PLANNING YOUR PROJECT

A full exterior update—or a specific area to address?

Before choosing a color, decide what you want the project to accomplish. You might be replacing worn siding, updating the look of your home, or finishing an addition. Tell us which parts of the property you want included so we can start with the right scope.

Whole-home replacement

Consider the house, attached garage and any other structures separately. Let us know whether your goal is a consistent new exterior or replacing only the existing siding.

One wall or a smaller area

Describe the area and what prompted the project. Matching an existing product and color can be a consideration; an initial conversation can help clarify whether the scope is a fit.

Siding for an addition

Share what is being built and which existing walls will remain. Decide whether you want the addition to match the house or use it as the starting point for a wider exterior update.

BEFORE YOU CHOOSE A CONTRACTOR

A useful starting point for your estimate

01 / YOUR PRIORITIES

Explain what you want changed

Your town, the areas of the home involved and your preferred timing are enough to begin. You can add a short description in the form; final product choices can come later.

02 / THE DETAILS

Ask what is included

When reviewing an estimate, ask about removal of existing siding, preparation, trim, cleanup and how any additional work would be discussed. These are questions to clarify, not assumed inclusions.

03 / YOUR DECISION

Compare the same scope

Look at the proposed materials, work included, exclusions and schedule together. Ask for clarification where two estimates differ so you understand what each price covers.

HOMEOWNER QUESTIONS

Planning siding in Saratoga Springs

What affects the scope of a siding replacement?

The area being covered, existing siding, access, trim details and any related work all belong in the conversation. Describe what you can see and what you want changed; you do not need to diagnose the cause before getting in touch.

Can I compare a full replacement with a smaller project?

Include both options in your request. Explain which areas matter most and ask whether each option fits the project. A smaller area can raise questions about matching the siding that stays.

Do I need to choose materials before I contact Vermac?

No. Start with your goals, your town and the parts of the home you want included. You can discuss appearance, maintenance preferences and budget before settling on a product.
