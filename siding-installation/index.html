Planning siding for a new home or an addition? Vermac Construction works with homeowners in Malta, Ballston Spa and across Saratoga County to plan an exterior that fits the home, the project and the budget.

Start with your home and your plans

A siding estimate should account for more than the visible panels. Tell us whether you are building a home, finishing an addition or updating an existing exterior. We can discuss the siding style, trim, wall layout and the work needed around windows, doors and corners.

If your home already has siding that needs to come off, visit our siding replacement page for what to consider before requesting an estimate.

What to include in your request

  • Your town and whether the project is a new home or an addition.
  • The look you want, including any siding or trim preferences.
  • Whether you have plans available and when you hope to start.
  • Other exterior work you would like to discuss at the same time.

Compare the complete scope

When reviewing siding proposals, ask what is included for wall preparation, moisture management, trim, material delivery and cleanup. Product choice and installation details both matter. Bring questions about maintenance, available colors and product warranties to your consultation so the scope is clear before you decide.
