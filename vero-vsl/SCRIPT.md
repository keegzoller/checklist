# Vero VSL: script and shot list

**Audience:** cold prospects, multi-location operators (3 to 5 units)
**Runtime:** 1:20 (2,400 frames at 30fps)
**Format:** 1920x1080
**Ask:** 15-minute walkthrough on their own accounts

The video is built to work **muted**. On-screen text carries the whole story, so it holds
up in a LinkedIn feed or an email embed before you ever record voiceover. The VO below is
written to sit on top of the existing timing without changing a single frame.

---

## Scene-by-scene

### 1. Hook (0:00 to 0:06)

**On screen:** FOR OPERATORS WITH 3 TO 5 LOCATIONS / *You researched every location before
you opened it.* / Demographics. Traffic patterns. Competitors. Where those customers
already went. / five location chips

**VO:** "You researched every location before you opened it. Demographics. Traffic.
Competitors."

*Note: this is the whole reason the pitch lands. They already believe every location is
different. You are just reminding them.*

---

### 2. The turn (0:06 to 0:13)

**On screen:** *Then you market all of them like they are* **one**. / One campaign. One
budget. One report that averages every store together. / one node fanning into five

**VO:** "Then you market all of them like they're one. One campaign, one budget, one report
that averages every store together."

---

### 3. The blind spot (0:13 to 0:22)

**On screen:** *So when one store is down and another is up* / South Tampa -18% / ? / West
Palm Beach +31% / *nobody can tell you why.*

**VO:** "So when one store is down and another is up, nobody can tell you why. Not your
agency. Not your ad platforms."

*Note: the ? beat is the emotional center of the video. Let it breathe. Do not rush into
the next line.*

---

### 4. Why the reports do not help (0:22 to 0:29)

**On screen:** WHAT YOUR REPORTS SHOW YOU / Impressions, Clicks, Cost per lead, Engagement
rate (each struck through) / *None of it tells you whether the* **register moved**.

**VO:** "You get impressions, clicks, cost per lead. None of it tells you whether the
register actually moved."

---

### 5. Vero enters (0:29 to 0:39) **REAL SCREEN**

**On screen:** THIS IS VERO / *Your register and your ad accounts,* **reconciled**. / Sources
are reconciled before anything is measured, so four platforms cannot each claim the same
sale. / Vero Connections page: Square, Meta, Google and Google Analytics feeding the Vero
measurement layer, out to Location performance, Channel measurement, Readiness controls

**VO:** "That's what Vero does. Your point of sale and every ad account feed one measurement
layer, reconciled before anything gets counted."

*Note: the reconciliation point is the one a skeptical operator actually cares about. Four
platforms all claiming the same sale is the thing they already suspect is happening.*

---

### 6. Ask it (0:39 to 0:49) **REAL SCREEN**

**On screen:** ASK, DO NOT DIG / *Ask it in* **plain English**. / Which locations are
performing best. Is the marketing actually working. Where should we invest next. / Vero
workspace: $4.9M revenue in view, 16 locations, 12/16 marketed, 26 week window, and the four
starter investigations

**VO:** "And you ask it in plain English. Which locations are performing best. Is the
marketing actually working. Where should we invest next."

---

### 7. The answer (0:49 to 1:02) **REAL SCREEN, then sample**

**On screen:** LOCATION LEVEL TRUTH / *It reads* **every location** *in the register.* / Ad
exposure matched against point of sale, store by store. / Vero investigating: "Reading every
location in the register," scope confirmed, reading source data, preparing the answer. Then
the answer itself, ranked by location.

**VO:** "It reads every location in the register, matches ad exposure against point of sale,
and comes back store by store. Same budget, same campaign, completely different answer."

*Note: the investigating state is real footage. The ranked answer that follows is a designed
sample, tagged as one on screen, until a real answer capture replaces it.*

---

### 8. The line (1:02 to 1:08)

**On screen:** *Every platform knows who your customers are.* / *Vero shows you* **where
they came from**.

**VO:** "Every platform knows who your customers are. Vero shows you where they came from."

---

### 9. Close (1:08 to 1:20)

**On screen:** *Connect your accounts. Look at* **your own numbers**. / Fifteen minutes, on
your locations, not a slide deck of someone else's. / VCS / www.vc-solutions.net / Keegan
Zoller, Founder, (813) 219-0955

**VO:** "If you run three to five locations, connect your accounts and look at your own
numbers. Fifteen minutes, on your locations, not a slide deck of someone else's."

## Recording notes

Record scene by scene, not in one pass. Nine separate takes drop into nine separate slots,
so a bad line costs you one retake instead of the whole read.

- Target pace is roughly 2.5 words per second. Every line above is written short on purpose
  so you have room to breathe rather than race the cut.
- Leave about half a second of silence at the head and tail of each take.
- Read scenes 1 through 4 flat and matter of fact. You are describing their situation, not
  selling yet. The energy lifts at scene 5 and stays up through the close.
- Phone voice memo in a carpeted room beats a nice mic in a kitchen. Get close, avoid the
  room echo.

---

## What is real and what is not

Three of the four product screens are real footage from your 2026-09-07 recording: the
Connections page, the workspace home, and the "reading every location in the register"
investigating state. The workspace name in the Connections header is blurred, and the "1 of
5 sources authorized" line is cropped out.

The fourth is a designed sample, because the recording ends before Vero renders an answer.
It carries a "sample view" tag on screen and it is styled from Vero's own palette so it does
not read as a different product.

**One capture finishes this.** Run the same investigation through to a rendered answer, grab
the frame, and it drops in: put the PNG in `public/screens/` and set `answer` in
`src/screens.ts`. Nothing else changes. Until then the payoff shot of the video is a
mockup, and a prospect who asks "is that real" on the call deserves a straight answer.

Every dollar figure in that sample is illustrative. The real numbers in the two workspace
screens ($4.9M in view, 16 locations) are yours and stay as they are.
