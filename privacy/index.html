Your Quote Request Information

When you request an estimate, we collect the information you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, town and project details. We use it to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project.

How requests are handled

Our website uses Netlify to host the site and store form submissions. Notifications can be sent to the business by email, and requests are also forwarded to our GoHighLevel customer contact system. These providers process the information needed to deliver those services.

We may include the page you submitted from, the landing page within your browser session and campaign labels from the link you followed so we can understand which pages bring inquiries. Campaign information is stored temporarily in your browser session. The form does not request payment information.

Contact us about your information

For questions or requests concerning information you sent us, email vermacconstruction1@gmail.com or call 518-636-8008.

Student program inquiries

Future Builders inquiries may include your connection to a student, school or community, contact details, and questions. We use this information to respond about the program. Please do not submit student records, birth dates, or other sensitive information through this form.
